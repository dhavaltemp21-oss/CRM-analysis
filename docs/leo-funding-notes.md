## What the feasibility grant covers (checked)

Sources: [Wicklow LEO feasibility grant](https://www.localenterprise.ie/wicklow/financial-supports/feasibility-grant/), [Carlow LEO application form](https://consult.carlow.ie/en/node/1239), [Donegal LEO feasibility grant](https://www.localenterprise.ie/donegal/financial-supports/feasibility-grant/).

- **Who qualifies.** Only businesses or individuals exploring a manufacturing or internationally traded service sector business. The enterprise must have no more than 10 employees and operate commercially.
- **Grant rate.** 60% of eligible spend in the BMW region (Border, Midlands, West) and 50% elsewhere, capped at €15,000 or the rate, whichever is less. Donegal is BMW, so it shows 60%. Wicklow and Carlow show 50%.
- **Quotes.** Three quotes are required for any single item over €5,000. Items under €5,000 need one verbal quote. The application also needs quotations for the key costs.
- **What can be claimed.** Prototype, innovation, design, consultancy and market research costs. Only third-party costs qualify. Own labour is capped at €400 a week and 20% of the grant. VAT is not covered.
- **Timing.** Nothing can be spent before the application. Payment is made after proof of payment, so the client may have to pay first and claim later.
- **Deliverables.** A written feasibility report is required for the claim.
- **Other conditions.** Applicants must meet a Business Advisor first, and the claim needs a valid tax clearance certificate.

## What "three quotes" means

When a client applies for a feasibility grant for a project costing over €5,000, they must include three written quotes from three different suppliers, for the same scope. We would be one of those three. The client may choose another supplier. The quote would need to be scoped to what the grant covers, which is prototype or feasibility work, not necessarily the full build.
